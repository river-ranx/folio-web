FAQ

Still curious?

Folio is a universal file viewer for Mac. One app opens documents, spreadsheets, slides, PDFs, notes, code, images, music and video — replacing the dozen apps you usually juggle, in one quiet, focused window.

macOS Preview is built for a quick glance. Folio is built to stay: tabs, cross-file search, in-place editing for Markdown and Word, and 15 reading themes — turning "peek and close" into "open and work".

Yes. Markdown, Word (.docx) and code files are editable in place, and .docx stays .docx. Other Office files and PDFs open for reading.

Folio supports macOS 15 and above.

Folio quietly prepares when you open a folder, then only catches up on changes. Even folders with 10,000 files respond as you type — you won't notice it working.

Viewing, searching and editing all happen on your Mac. Folio never uploads your files. In the full edition, cloud translation or an AI CLI only receives the text you choose to send.

The Mac App Store version is free to download.

It is a separate build distributed directly, outside the Mac App Store. It includes a terminal, Git, AI coding CLIs, tasks, automatic snapshots and more translation back-ends. The App Store version does not include the terminal, Git, AI CLIs or tasks. No release date or price has been announced yet.
