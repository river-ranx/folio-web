FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do I need to run it? Is there a Windows or iPhone version?

Both editions need macOS 15 or later, and Folio Pro is built for Apple silicon Macs. Windows, iPadOS and iOS versions are on the way.

Does it cost anything?

Both Folio and Folio Pro are free for now.

Folio or Folio Pro — which should I pick?

If you mostly open files and make quick edits to Markdown, Word or code, pick Folio from the Mac App Store. If you also want a terminal, Git, AI CLIs and automatic versions in the same window, pick Folio Pro. Both share the same viewing and editing foundation.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Viewing, searching and editing happen on your Mac. In Folio Pro, translation has four engines: Apple system translation and local Ollama stay on your Mac, while Gemini CLI and Google Cloud send the text being translated to the cloud — and the interface labels which is which. The AI CLI hub reads just the session history already on your Mac; Folio doesn’t parse or store messages.

Will editing a .docx mess up its formatting?

No. It’s .docx in, .docx out: paragraph styles, tables, images and watermarks are preserved, and what you see while editing is the final document.

How do I install Folio Pro?

Download Folio-Pro.zip from this page, unzip it and drag Folio Pro into Applications. It’s signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, and it isn’t distributed through the Mac App Store.

Can I install both editions?

Yes. They’re two separate apps and each keeps its own settings.
